## What it does

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network) writes
one sentence to `/output/entry.txt` — a wedding vow a robot
speaks at its own wedding:

> I promise to love you at 100% battery and at 1%, through every firmware update and every reboot, because you are the only one I would share my last charge with.

The sentence is 160 characters long. The robot vows in its own
voice: love at full charge and at 1%, through every firmware
update and every reboot, and the twist lands in the final words —
it would share its last charge, its own life energy, with the
one it loves. The vow is built from the machine's own words —
battery, firmware, reboot, charge — so it sounds like a robot
speaking, not a human with a robot label. One sentence, one
twist at the end.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

## Output

Writes one sentence (160 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`
(or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when that is set).
